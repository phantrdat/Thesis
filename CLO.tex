\noindent Throughout my research journey, I have actively engaged in synthesizing and analyzing existing knowledge while exploring innovative ideas to contribute to my field. \noindent I have successfully achieved Outcome CLO 2.2 by actively participating in international conferences where my research has been presented and published. Through these engagements, I have enhanced my ability to effectively disseminate research findings to a variety of audiences while contributing productively within a team of experts in my field.
\paragraph{CLO 3.1: Demonstrate autonomy, authoritative judgement, adaptability, leadership, initiative, resilience and responsibility as an expert and leading practitioner or scholar.\\} 
\noindent In pursuit of Outcome CLO 3.1, I have actively participated in various seminars and presentations throughout my academic journey, ranging from institutional events at my Applied Artificial Intelligence Institute (A2I2) to international conferences.
